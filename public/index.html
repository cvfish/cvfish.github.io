Rui Yu

I am a Staff Engineer at Apple. I work on 3D computer vision and machine learning, with a particular interest in understanding the geometry and motion of the world from images and video.

Bio

Before Apple, I worked on 3D vision at DJI. I completed my PhD at University College London, advised by Lourdes Agapito and Chris Russell. Earlier, I studied at Northwestern Polytechnical University, working with Yanning Zhang and Tao Yang.

You can find more about my experience and education in my CV (PDF).
